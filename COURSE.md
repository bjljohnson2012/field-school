# Field School — Grok Bot vs OpenClaw and Hermes

A bottom-up mini-course from [Ray Fernando’s live walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAoTrUijP4g) (video `sAoTrUijP4g`, ~74 minutes).

**How to pass:** watch the clip for each station, finish the required field work, score 75% on the station quiz. Then 8/10 on the exam.

You do not need Cursor Ultra to do the field work. You need it to run the real staff.

## The ladder

1. Name the work (not the tool)
2. One computer that stays signed in
3. One bot, one job
4. Three plugins, one failure mode
5. Teach a five-click skill
6. Give it a clock (routine + nag)
7. Let specialists argue in one thread
8. Ship from chat (data → dashboard → app)
9. Overnight potato brief (quality, not slop)
10. One business use case + a kill switch

### Station 01 — What this actually is
[0:00–2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAoTrUijP4g&t=0s)

Grok Bot is named teammates with jobs, a phone + desktop iMessage surface, and a cloud computer that keeps working when your laptop is closed. Access in the stream: Cursor Ultra (~$200) or SuperGrok Heavy (~$300).

**Field work:** list 8 tasks you already “text a colleague” about. Star 3. Write the top outcome in one sentence.

### Station 02 — The two computers
[1:04–2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAoTrUijP4g&t=64s) · [27:42–28:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAoTrUijP4g&t=1662s)

OpenClaw/Hermes tax: every new agent re-signs into Chrome. Grok Bot shares cookies on one cloud desktop and can still SSH your Tailscale machines.

**Field work:** 5 accounts the cloud box must hold. 2 things that stay local. Why the job dies if it only runs while the laptop is open.

### Station 03 — Hire one bot, one job
[0:20–1:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAoTrUijP4g&t=20s) · [43:48–45:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAoTrUijP4g&t=2628s)

Plus button. Describe the job. Do not start with a fleet.

**Field work:** coworker name, five-line job (done / never), first message.

### Station 04 — Plugins and MCP
[5:14–5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAoTrUijP4g&t=314s) · [9:28–10:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAoTrUijP4g&t=568s)

Plugins are the business layer. Withings was not in the catalog — the bot found the MCP and logged in through the browser. Vercel is how dashboards and apps leave the chat.

**Field work:** three plugins. Failure if #1 is missing. Personal vs business identity.

### Station 05 — Teach Task
[17:13–18:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAoTrUijP4g&t=1033s)

Record a click-path. Get a skill file. Ticket Snagger: photo of a parking ticket, form filled, human pays.

**Field work:** five numbered clicks, the one step a human still does.

### Station 06 — Routines
[10:12–11:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAoTrUijP4g&t=612s) · [26:33–27:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAoTrUijP4g&t=1593s)

Scheduled wake-ups, per bot. Ray’s agent wrote its own weigh-in / Sunday review instructions.

**Field work:** name / when / done. Exact nag copy. Weekly review that reads memory.

### Station 07 — A staff in one thread
[6:48–13:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAoTrUijP4g&t=408s) · [13:18–14:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAoTrUijP4g&t=798s)

General staff + researcher + recomp negotiate a widget from a week of memory. Same thread on the phone. You can leave.

**Field work:** three bots, a handoff message, one veto.

### Station 08 — Ship from the chat
[2:20–6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAoTrUijP4g&t=140s) · [53:22–56:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAoTrUijP4g&t=3202s)

Luma: 187 guests, 32 lightning talks. Spreadsheet → dashboard → QR app (Next.js, Convex, Clerk) via Cursor cloud + P-Stack. Human leftover: keys.

**Field work:** who visits, where data already lives, stack, three refusals for v0.

### Station 09 — Potato mode
[21:57–25:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAoTrUijP4g&t=1317s) · [49:32–51:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAoTrUijP4g&t=2972s)

Lauren’s P-Stack: go deep first, then parallel. “Potato mode, I’m going to bed.” ~4 hours, ~25 commits. Trio: Grok, GPT-Sol to verify, Composer for speed.

**Field work:** source of truth, four-bullet quality bar, who verifies.

### Station 10 — Run it as a business
[30:01–31:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAoTrUijP4g&t=1801s) · [59:17–1:01:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAoTrUijP4g&t=3557s)

Eric’s use cases (ICP screen, competitor prices, “what did we promise”). $200 vs forty hours of clicking. Keep a human on send / pay / approve. Beta is still beta.

**Field work:** score three use cases. Pick one for 14 days. Write the kill switch.
