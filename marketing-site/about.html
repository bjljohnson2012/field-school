About

Field School is the organization.

The training portal, the Saturday newsletter, the community, and coaching all sit under it. I built one home for that work instead of four disconnected products.

The work is the same in each: AI you can use, sales and go-to-market that close, leadership that builds for the future, and small moves you can make with your family.

Training portal

Courses, assessments, and certificates. You enroll and learn at your own pace. The first course is The Twelve Presuppositions. One course is free.

Newsletter

One Saturday email. Use it the same week. Free. Most people meet the work here first.

Community

People dedicated to learning. Build things that last. Stay ready. Not a feed.

Coaching

Online, in the room, or one hour a week with Ben. Direct feedback on the work you are doing. Those seats include the newsletter and the portal.
