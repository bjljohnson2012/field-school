What we want

The community

LyellX exists to build companies that help a community get better at the work in front of them. Not another feed. Shops people actually use, in the week they are in.

Practical AI

Skills you can put on a live job. AI as a teammate with a clear job, not a tab you forget by Sunday.

Sales and GTM that close

Systems for rooms with real constraints. Compliance, budget cycles, career risk. Playbooks that survive those rooms.

Leadership that builds

Leadership that builds for the future. Teams from zero. Repeatable process instead of a heroic week.

Home, not only work

Practical moves people can make with their families. The week has to still work when the laptop shuts.

How it shows up

Field School is one public door: courses, assessments, certificates, a participant community, a Saturday newsletter, and coaching with Ben.

Problem-solving first

LyellX lives by first principles. We build and we expand. Consulting, training, speaking, revenue operations, and go-to-market growth stay on the same method.

The community we want is people who get better at the work that is arriving, without quitting their job or buying every tool on day one.
