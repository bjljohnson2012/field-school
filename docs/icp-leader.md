# ICP: leader

Room: team. Launch stays closed. This file is not a launch score. Team is not a later step of the household. It is the other room.

## Hirer

The leader. The leader is the User. The leader pays and owns the intent, the path, and any lock or override. The leader is accountable for the people and for the book of business.

## Org

The sales team. The shop, or the book of business, is the organization that has to get better.

## Person in development

The teammate. The teammate may sign in to do the work. The teammate does not buy and does not own the path. The teammate is not a child.

## Job

When I am accountable for people's development and for the organization's success, and I cannot sit with them every hour, I invest in Field School so each person keeps moving on a path fit to who they are now, they get better, the organization gets better, and the learning actually takes.

## Anti-job

Refused: person-in-development as buyer, child login, fourth SKU, public-site-first. Also refused: a separate team price, a district or HR seat dump, and quiz-as-product. The household amounts $100, $200, and $1,000 a month are not a new menu for this room. No live card.

## Trigger in force

The next step is missing or untrustworthy, and development stalls when the leader leaves the room.

## Who we get after

1. The same leader, in the same team, at a higher intensity.
2. The same leader, with a second teammate who can sign in and still does not buy.
3. Another leader whose team already looks like this one.
4. An operator of many orgs, only after this room keeps moving when the leader is absent. That operator is a later file.

## Current-state fit

The path uses what is already in hand: intent, progress, the existing Field Pattern profile, skill, and what the teammate just did. No second item bank. No official psychometric text. The sales desk does not show a child.

## Quote

"I need them to keep moving when I am not on the call."
