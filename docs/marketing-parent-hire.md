# Parent hire

Room: household. The parent is the user. Public site stays unflipped. This file is not a public page.

## The job

When I am accountable for people's development and for the organization's success, and I cannot sit with them every hour, I invest in Field School so each person keeps moving on a path fit to who they are now, they get better, the organization gets better, and the learning actually takes.

## Three prices

Learn with Ben is three monthly amounts on the parent account. Homeschool is a mode on that account. No other hire price belongs here.

| Amount | Seat | Hour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $100 a month | Online cohort | One hour a week online. |
| $200 a month | In the room | The same hour, in person. |
| $1,000 a month | One-on-one hour | One hour a week with Ben. |

## Portal

The parent signs in at https://portal.fieldschool.ai.

## Child as a progress record

The child is a progress record (`kind:child`, `login:none`, `user:false`). The child holds no email, no password, and no session. The parent owns intent, the path, and the progress on that record.

## What the parent does next

Development stalls when the parent leaves the room and the child is waiting on the next portion. The parent signs in at the portal, hires one amount ($100, $200, or $1,000 a month), selects that child record, and locks the next portion so the path keeps moving when the parent is not in the room.
