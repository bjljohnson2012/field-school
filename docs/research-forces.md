# Research forces

C3. Feeds ICP in `docs/icp-parent.md` and `docs/icp-leader.md`. Launch stays closed. This file keeps the public claim closed.

## Job

When I am accountable for people's development and for the organization's success, and I cannot sit with them every hour, I invest in Field School so each person keeps moving on a path fit to who they are now, they get better, the organization gets better, and the learning actually takes.

ICP may sharpen that sentence. This file keeps it.

Rooms in this file: household and team. Household is the family. Team is the book of business, the shop. Each room has its own hirer and its own person in development.

The hirer is the User. The person in development is the one who gets better. The next portion still runs when the hirer leaves the room. Sign-in for the hirer stays at https://portal.fieldschool.ai.

## Household

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Hirer | Parent. The User. Owns intent, path, lock, and override. |
| Organization | The family. |
| Person in development | Child. `kind:child`, `login:none`, `user:false`. A progress record on the parent User. |
| Offer in force | Learn with Ben at $100 / $200 / $1,000 a month. Homeschool is a mode on that same parent User. |

### Push

The parent is accountable for the child's development and for the household, and cannot sit with the child every hour. The next step is missing or untrustworthy. Development stalls when the parent leaves. The household feels that stall.

### Pull

The child keeps moving on a path fit to current state already in hand: intent, progress, the living profile, skill, and what the child just did. The child gets better. The household gets better. The learning takes. The parent still owns the path. The next portion is available without a new conversation.

### Anxiety

The platform starts acting as the parent. A generic catalog replaces a path. A quiz becomes the product. A new amount appears beside $100, $200, and $1,000. A card is charged from a note. A public claim goes out while launch stays closed. The child is treated as the buyer. An official psychometric bank is treated as the path. Those worries stop the hire. This file leaves them unanswered by a new feature.

### Habit

The current way is worksheets, hope, an unstructured week, another deck, an extra tutor amount, or a message that the course was sent. The parent stays in every hour because nothing else moves. Name that habit. Leave it outside the product.

### Who we get after

Inside the household, in this order:

1. The same parent, the same household, at a higher intensity of the hour already priced.
2. The same parent, a second child, still a progress record on the parent User.
3. Another parent whose household already looks like this room.
4. An operator of many households, and only after this room's loop still runs when the parent is absent. That operator is a later ICP file.
