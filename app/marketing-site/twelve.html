How we see the work

12 Presuppositions

These are the rules we will not treat as optional. The courses, the Saturday email, and the hour with Ben use them. You still have to use them on a Tuesday when no one is there to tell you the next move.

  1. Everything Is Made Up

    People wrote the rules. God wrote the world. Do not treat a policy like gravity, or gravity like a policy.

  2. You Own Your Response

    The freeze gets a vote. It does not get the last word. The next move is yours.

  3. Reality > Hopeum

    The world keeps its own books. Look first. Then interpret. Hope does not close a deal.

  4. Ego Destroys Kingdoms

    Certainty about yourself is the blindness you cannot see. Stay a student on purpose, especially when you are winning.

  5. Working In vs. Working On

    Work inside the system. Also step out long enough to see what it is for.

  6. No Game Is Played on Neutral Ground

    No game is played on neutral ground. Name the players and what each is rewarded for before the next move.

  7. Nothing Exists in Isolation

    Nothing exists in isolation. Name the kind of system you are in before you act.

  8. Information Must Be Interpreted, Not Just Received

    People rarely say exactly what they mean. How they act is data. Notice, guess carefully, then ask. Listen under the words.

  9. People Move When Value Outweighs Risk

    People move when the good looks bigger than the scare. Talk about the whole pile, not only the price.

  10. You Cannot Reason From What You Do Not Understand

    You cannot reason from what you do not understand. A recommendation is only as strong as the model under it.

  11. To Advise Is to Share in the Decision

    A recommendation is a judgment someone may act on. Do not just hand over facts. Help them know what to do.

  12. You must READ THE ROOM

    People show the defense they use to feel safe, not who they are. Read the fear under the behavior. Use it for compassion, not as a tool.
