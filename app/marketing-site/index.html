Field School

It is for people who still have a number, a family, and a week that has to move.

Ben Johnson, Founder

The week is full. The work still has to move.

You still have a number. You still have a family. The tools changed again this month. Tactics are still firing, the calendar is still full, and the thing you meant to get better at is still waiting for a quieter week. AI gets tried once and dropped. Sales advice stays on a slide. Leadership gets postponed until the quarter is quiet. The week did not get easier because you collected another tab.

You are expected to get better, with no place to practice.

Most of what is sold as learning is a feed or a pile of videos. You are supposed to form a point of view, run the meeting, and come home as the same person, with nowhere to pressure-test the move before you make it. Field School is where you get better: learning, developing, and AI-powered knowledge, in a course, a Saturday note, a community, and an hour with Ben.
