They are not getting better when you are not there.

The number is still on the board. The family still gets you at night. The person you meant to develop is in another meeting, waiting on an hour you do not have. You tried the model on Tuesday. By Friday it was a tab. The sales advice is still a slide. Leadership is still waiting on a quiet quarter. Nothing moved because you saved another link.

A feed is not a path.

Most of what is sold as learning asks you to watch, feel briefly current, and meet the same Monday. Field School is the other arrangement. Learning, developing, and AI-powered knowledge, fit to the person in front of you. A station you can finish. A Saturday note you can use the same week. People who are doing the work, not performing it. An hour with Ben when the work needs a human. Then you close the laptop. The next step is still waiting for them.
