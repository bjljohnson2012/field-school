Field School

It is for people who still have a number, a family, and a week that has to move.

Ben Johnson, Founder

People do not get better on the days you are not with them.

The number is still on the board. Your family still sees you at night, after the work. The person you meant to teach is in another meeting, waiting for an hour you do not have. You tried an AI tool on Tuesday. By Friday you had closed the tab. The sales advice is still a slide. You put off leadership until the quarter is quiet. Saving another link did not change any of that.

Watching a video is not practice.

A lot of what is sold as learning asks you to watch, feel up to date for an afternoon, and walk into the same Monday. Field School is the practice. You learn a skill. You help a person get better at it. You write down what they know so an AI can repeat the parts that should be repeated, and a person still checks the result. A lesson you can finish. A Saturday email you can use the same week. Other people doing the same work. One hour with Ben when you need someone to look at the work with you. When you close the laptop, the next assignment is still waiting for them.
