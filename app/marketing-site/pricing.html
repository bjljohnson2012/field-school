Pricing

Start free. Pay when you want more.

Field School is learning, developing, and AI-powered knowledge. The newsletter is free. One training portal course is free. A coaching price includes both, so you are not paying only for the hour with Ben. Paid prices take a card.

  • Start with the Saturday email, or with one free course.

  • Pay for an hour with Ben when you want a person looking at the work: $100 online, $200 in Dayton, Ohio, or $1,000 one to one. Farther from Dayton, you cover your own travel and stay.
