What is a knowledge brain?

Everything your people need to know, written down once. AI handles the repeat work. You still make the calls.

Is this for families or for teams?

Both. One school. Two rooms. A family and a team do not share a desk.

Is homeschool a separate product?

No. It is a mode inside the family room.

What happens in a session with Ben?

Ben sits with you for an hour, takes in how the work runs, and builds the knowledge brain with you. You leave with a recap and the next step in the portal.
